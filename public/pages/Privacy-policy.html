Privacy Policy

Last updated 01 October 2026. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

MARIS & CATCH Purveyors Ltd (“MARIS & CATCH”, “we”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy covers the marisandcatch.com website, our ordering systems and any related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly and information gathered automatically as you use our services.

  • Account details — name, email address and password when you register.
  • Order details — delivery address, phone number, order history and preferences.
  • Payment data — processed by our PCI-compliant payment partners; we never store raw card numbers.
  • Usage data — pages visited, device type and approximate location for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to fulfil orders, manage subscriptions, provide customer support and improve our service. Specifically, we rely on data to:

  • Process payments and deliver your orders accurately.
  • Send transactional messages such as order confirmations and dispatch alerts.
  • Send marketing communications only where you have opted in.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with trusted partners who help us operate, and only to the extent necessary — including delivery drivers, payment processors and cloud hosting providers. All partners are bound by confidentiality and data-protection agreements.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use a small number of cookies and local storage keys to keep you signed in, remember your theme and language preferences, and measure site performance. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings; essential cookies are required for checkout.

5. Data Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to perform their role, and is protected by multi-factor authentication. We retain order records only as long as required for tax and legal purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Contact & Changes

If you have questions about this policy, email privacy@marisandcatch.com or write to Privacy Team, MARIS & CATCH Purveyors Ltd, Pier 4, Harbor Basin, Coastal Terminal 2. We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be announced on this page.
